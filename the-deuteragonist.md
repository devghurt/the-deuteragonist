# The Deuteragonist
**Deuteragonist**
> noun - the character next in importance to the protagonist, esp the antagonist

## PROLOGUE
	"Who are you, really?"\
	The wind gave no reply. As expected.\
	It had been hours since his last contact with this enigmatic voice that would whisper lies, propaganda and incredulous hypotheticals into his 'measly mortal brain'. Everytime, he would bang his head onto his writing desk to make it *stop stop stop*. It proved effective. For a while. Then it would start again, a never-ending cycle of teasing, mockery and pestering.\
	He swallowed a gulp, his throat dry and scratchy.\
	"I don't expect you to give me any answer, but the least you could do is save these poor souls from what's coming next."\
	Radio silence. As expected.\
	"Fine." The guy clenched his perpetually ink-stained hands. "If you want me to be your villain, I will be."\
	*I'm sick and tired of this life anyway.*
	It hurt to be the only one in the room, no -- the only one in the world who understood the inner workings of his universe, like some self-aware character in a TV show who occasionally breaks the fourth wall for the sake of comedy.\
	"I don't know what you're talking about," he mumbles in a strained voice. Then in a barely audible tone, he adds, "I wish I'd never touched a pen and paper in my life."\
	*Go*, a silky voice in his head coaxes, *ruin their lives.*\
	This time, he doesn't disobey.
